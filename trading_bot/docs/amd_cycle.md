# AMD Cycle (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution)

The AMD cycle, also known as **Power of 3**, is the fundamental market cycle that smart money uses to accumulate and distribute positions.

## The Three Phases

### 1. Accumulation Phase

**What it is:** Smart money quietly builds positions during low volatility periods.

**Characteristics:**
- Occurs during Asian session (7 PM - 12 AM EST)
- Price consolidates in a tight range
- Low volume, minimal volatility
- Range often forms around NY midnight open
- Both buyers and sellers enter positions

**What to do:**
- Mark the accumulation range (high and low)
- Do NOT trade during this phase
- Wait for manipulation to begin

### 2. Manipulation Phase (Judas Swing)

**What it is:** A false move designed to trigger retail stops and trap traders on the wrong side.

**Characteristics:**
- Occurs at London open (2-3 AM EST) or NY open (7-8 AM EST)
- Price breaks the accumulation range in the OPPOSITE direction of the true move
- Sweeps liquidity (stop losses) above/below the range
- Creates a "false breakout" that traps retail traders
- Usually quick and aggressive (1-3 candles on M15)

**The Judas Swing:**
- Named after the betrayal - the market "lies" about its true direction
- In a bullish day: Judas swing goes DOWN first
- In a bearish day: Judas swing goes UP first
- This is where smart money completes accumulation at better prices

**What to do:**
- DO NOT chase the manipulation move
- Wait for price to return inside the range
- Look for reversal signals (MSS, order blocks)

### 3. Distribution Phase

**What it is:** The true directional move where smart money distributes positions to late retail traders.

**Characteristics:**
- Occurs after manipulation completes
- Strong, sustained move in the true direction
- High volume, expanding candles
- Often reaches previous day high/low or higher TF targets
- This is where you take profits

**What to do:**
- Enter during late manipulation or early distribution
- Ride the move with trailing stops
- Take profits at liquidity targets (PDH, PDL, equal highs/lows)

## Identifying the Phases

### Accumulation Identification
```
Criteria:
1. Time: Asian session
2. Price action: Tight range, small candles
3. Volume: Low to moderate
4. Range: Usually 20-40 pips for majors
```

### Manipulation Identification
```
Criteria:
1. Time: Session open (London or NY)
2. Price action: Aggressive move breaking range
3. Liquidity: Sweeps obvious highs/lows
4. Speed: Fast, 1-3 candles
5. Direction: OPPOSITE to daily bias
```

### Distribution Identification
```
Criteria:
1. Time: After manipulation completes
2. Price action: Strong momentum, displacement
3. Structure: Market structure shift (MSS)
4. Direction: Same as daily bias
5. Volume: Increasing
```

## Trading the AMD Cycle

### Entry Strategy

1. **Mark accumulation range** during Asian session
2. **Determine bias** using higher timeframe analysis
3. **Wait for manipulation** (Judas swing)
4. **Confirm manipulation complete:**
   - Price sweeps liquidity
   - MSS occurs (breaks structure opposite to Judas)
   - Returns inside or near accumulation range
5. **Enter on pullback** to OB or FVG formed during MSS
6. **Stop loss** below manipulation low (bullish) or above manipulation high (bearish)
7. **Target** previous day extremes or higher TF liquidity

### Example: Bullish AMD Setup

```
Asian Session (Accumulation):
- Range forms: 1.0850 - 1.0870
- Midnight open: 1.0860
- Bias: Bullish (above key HTF level)

London Open (Manipulation):
- 2:15 AM: Price drops to 1.0840 (sweeps Asian low)
- Triggers buy stops placed below 1.0850
- This is the Judas swing (bearish fake)

Reversal (MSS):
- 2:45 AM: Price breaks back above 1.0850
- Forms bullish order block at 1.0845
- Market structure shifts bullish

Entry:
- Buy at 1.0848 (OB retest)
- Stop: 1.0835 (below manipulation low)
- Target: 1.0920 (PDH)
```

## Common AMD Mistakes

1. **Trading the manipulation** - Entering in the Judas swing direction
2. **Missing accumulation** - Not marking the range
3. **Premature entry** - Entering before MSS confirms
4. **Wrong bias** - Fighting the HTF direction
5. **Tight stops** - Placing stops inside manipulation range
