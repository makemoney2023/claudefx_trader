# Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)

## Overview

Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) is a precision entry technique using Fibonacci retracement levels to identify the best entry point during a pullback. The OTE zone is the 62-79% retracement area, with the 70.5% level being the "sweet spot."

## Fibonacci Levels in ICT

### Standard Retracement Levels
| Level | Significance |
|-------|-------------|
| 0% | Start of move (swing point) |
| 23.6% | Shallow retracement |
| 38.2% | Moderate retracement |
| **50%** | **Equilibrium (fair value)** |
| **61.8%** | **OTE zone start** |
| **70.5%** | **OTE sweet spot** |
| **79%** | **OTE zone end** |
| 100% | Full retracement |

### OTE Zone: 62-79%
The OTE zone is where price offers the best risk-reward for entries:
- **Top (62%)**: Conservative entry
- **Sweet spot (70.5%)**: Optimal entry
- **Bottom (79%)**: Aggressive entry (tighter stop)

## Premium vs Discount

### Premium Zone (Above 50%)
- Price is "expensive" relative to the range
- **Look to SELL/SHORT**
- Institutions distribute positions here
- Retail typically buying (wrong side)

### Discount Zone (Below 50%)
- Price is "cheap" relative to the range
- **Look to BUY/LONG**
- Institutions accumulate positions here
- Retail typically selling (wrong side)

### Equilibrium (50%)
- Fair value level
- No clear advantage to either side
- Often acts as magnet for price

## How to Use OTE

### For Bullish Setups
1. Identify swing high and swing low
2. Draw Fibonacci from swing LOW to swing HIGH
3. Wait for price to retrace DOWN into OTE zone (62-79%)
4. Look for bullish confirmation at OTE
5. Enter long with stop below 79% or swing low
6. Target: Previous high or higher

```
Swing High ─────────── 0%
                       │
                       │ Retracement
                       │
OTE Zone   ─────────── 62% (Entry zone top)
          │            │
          │  ENTRY →   │ 70.5% (Sweet spot)
          │            │
           ─────────── 79% (Entry zone bottom)
                       │
Swing Low  ─────────── 100%
```

### For Bearish Setups
1. Identify swing high and swing low
2. Draw Fibonacci from swing HIGH to swing LOW
3. Wait for price to retrace UP into OTE zone (62-79%)
4. Look for bearish confirmation at OTE
5. Enter short with stop above 79% or swing high
6. Target: Previous low or lower

## OTE + ICT Confluence

### OTE + Order Block
When OTE overlaps with an Order Block:
- Highest probability entry
- Use OB for precise entry level
- Strong institutional presence

### OTE + FVG
When OTE contains a Fair Value Gap:
- Enter at FVG within OTE
- Double confirmation
- Tighter stop possible

### OTE + Liquidity
OTE after liquidity sweep:
- Price swept stops then retraced
- Enter at OTE for continuation
- Very high probability

## Practical Example

### Bullish OTE Trade

1. **Setup**:
   - Swing Low: 1.0800
   - Swing High: 1.0900
   - Range: 100 pips

2. **Calculate Levels**:
   - 0% = 1.0900
   - 50% = 1.0850 (equilibrium)
   - 62% = 1.0838 (OTE top)
   - 70.5% = 1.0829 (sweet spot)
   - 79% = 1.0821 (OTE bottom)
   - 100% = 1.0800

3. **Trade**:
   - Wait for price to retrace to 1.0830 area
   - Look for bullish confirmation
   - Enter long at 1.0829
   - Stop loss: 1.0795 (below swing low)
   - Take profit: 1.0900+ (previous high or higher)

## Entry Confirmation at OTE

Don't blindly enter at OTE. Look for:

1. **Candlestick patterns**
   - Bullish engulfing
   - Pin bar / hammer
   - Morning star

2. **Structure shift**
   - LTF CHoCH in direction of trade
   - Break of micro structure

3. **FVG reaction**
   - Price respects FVG within OTE
   - Displacement away from OTE

4. **Order Block presence**
   - OB within OTE zone
   - Enter at OB touch

## Stop Loss Placement

### Conservative
- Below/above the OTE zone (79% level)
- Larger stop, more room

### Standard
- Below/above swing low/high
- Balanced approach

### Aggressive
- Just below/above the 79% level
- Tighter stop, higher risk of stop out

## Take Profit Targets

Using Fibonacci extensions:

| Target | Level |
|--------|-------|
| TP1 | Previous swing point (100%) |
| TP2 | -27% extension |
| TP3 | -62% extension |
| TP4 | -100% extension |

## Common Mistakes

1. **Entering before price reaches OTE**
   - Be patient, wait for the zone

2. **No confirmation at OTE**
   - Don't blindly enter, need confirmation

3. **Wrong trend direction**
   - OTE must align with HTF bias

4. **Drawing Fib incorrectly**
   - Bullish: Low to High
   - Bearish: High to Low

5. **Ignoring other confluence**
   - OTE alone isn't enough
   - Combine with OB, FVG, liquidity

## Best Practices

1. **Draw from significant swings**
   - Clear swing highs/lows
   - Not minor fluctuations

2. **Use with trend**
   - Bullish OTE in uptrends
   - Bearish OTE in downtrends

3. **Wait for the zone**
   - Patience is key
   - Don't chase price

4. **Confirm before entry**
   - LTF confirmation
   - Rejection candle
   - Structure break

5. **Respect the levels**
   - If price closes through 79%, setup may be invalid
   - Reassess the trade
