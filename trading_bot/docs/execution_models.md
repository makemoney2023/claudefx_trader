## 1. The Unicorn Model

The highest probability ICT setup combining multiple confluences.

### Definition
A Unicorn occurs when:
- A **Breaker Block** forms (failed order block)
- A **Fair Value Gap** overlaps the breaker
- Creates a "unicorn" zone for entry

### Formation Process

```
BULLISH UNICORN:

1. Bullish OB forms → Price rallies from OB
2. Price returns, BREAKS the OB low → OB becomes Breaker
3. Price continues down, creates FVG
4. FVG overlaps with the Breaker Block zone
5. UNICORN ZONE = Where Breaker and FVG overlap

Entry: Buy when price retraces to Unicorn zone
Stop: Below the FVG / Breaker low
Target: Previous high / liquidity
```

```
BEARISH UNICORN:

1. Bearish OB forms → Price drops from OB
2. Price returns, BREAKS the OB high → OB becomes Breaker
3. Price continues up, creates FVG
4. FVG overlaps with the Breaker Block zone
5. UNICORN ZONE = Where Breaker and FVG overlap

Entry: Sell when price retraces to Unicorn zone
Stop: Above the FVG / Breaker high
Target: Previous low / liquidity
```

### Unicorn Entry Rules

1. **Identify failed OB** (order block that gets broken)
2. **Mark the Breaker** (the failed OB area)
3. **Wait for FVG** to form overlapping the Breaker
4. **Entry at overlap zone** (50% of overlap ideal)
5. **Stop outside the structure** (below FVG low or above FVG high)

### Why Unicorns Work

- Breaker = Trapped traders creating demand/supply
- FVG = Institutional imbalance to be filled
- Overlap = Double reason for price reaction
- High confluence = Higher probability

## 2. The 2022 Model

A complete intraday execution model taught in ICT 2022 mentorship.

### Core Components

1. **Higher Timeframe Bias** (Daily/4H)
2. **Kill Zone Timing** (London or NY)
3. **Liquidity Sweep** (Judas swing)
4. **Market Structure Shift** (MSS)
5. **Entry at OB/FVG** (formed during MSS)

### 2022 Model Sequence

```
BULLISH 2022 MODEL:

[STEP 1] HTF Bias = Bullish
         - Daily showing demand
         - Price above weekly open

[STEP 2] Asian Range Forms
         - Mark high and low
         - Note liquidity below low

[STEP 3] Kill Zone Begins (London/NY)
         - Watch for manipulation

[STEP 4] Liquidity Sweep
         - Price breaks Asian low
         - Sweeps sell stops
         - This is the Judas swing

[STEP 5] Market Structure Shift
         - Price breaks back above recent swing high
         - Confirms reversal
         - Note the OB/FVG formed

[STEP 6] Entry
         - Price retraces to OB or FVG
         - Enter long position
         - Stop below sweep low

[STEP 7] Target
         - Asian high (first target)
         - Previous day high (second target)
         - Weekly high (extended target)
```

### 2022 Model Checklist

```
[ ] HTF bias determined (Daily chart)
[ ] Asian session range marked
[ ] In kill zone (London 2-5 AM or NY 7-10 AM EST)
[ ] Liquidity swept (low or high broken)
[ ] MSS confirmed (structure break opposite to sweep)
[ ] OB or FVG identified at MSS
[ ] Entry at OB/FVG
[ ] Stop below/above liquidity sweep
[ ] Target at next liquidity level
[ ] R:R minimum 2:1
```

## 3. PD Array Entry Model

Using Premium/Discount arrays for precision entries.

### Premium vs Discount

**Discount Zone (Below 50%):**
- Area below equilibrium
- Where smart money BUYS
- Look for LONG entries here

**Premium Zone (Above 50%):**
- Area above equilibrium
- Where smart money SELLS
- Look for SHORT entries here

### PD Array Components

Arrays in order of priority:

**For Longs (in Discount):**
1. Bullish Order Block
2. Bullish Breaker
3. Bullish Mitigation Block
4. Bullish FVG
5. Bullish Rejection Block

**For Shorts (in Premium):**
1. Bearish Order Block
2. Bearish Breaker
3. Bearish Mitigation Block
4. Bearish FVG
5. Bearish Rejection Block

### PD Array Stacking

Higher probability when arrays stack:

```
HIGHEST PROBABILITY LONG:
- Price in discount zone (below 50%)
- At bullish order block
- OB overlaps with FVG
- FVG overlaps with OTE (61.8-79%)
- Time is in kill zone

= Maximum confluence for entry
```

## 4. Turtle Soup Entry

Classic liquidity sweep entry technique.

### Turtle Soup Rules

**Bullish Turtle Soup:**
1. Previous low is clearly visible (liquidity)
2. Price breaks below that low (sweep)
3. Price CLOSES back above the low
4. Enter on close or next candle
5. Stop below the sweep wick
6. Target previous high

**Bearish Turtle Soup:**
1. Previous high is clearly visible (liquidity)
2. Price breaks above that high (sweep)
3. Price CLOSES back below the high
4. Enter on close or next candle
5. Stop above the sweep wick
6. Target previous low

### Turtle Soup Confirmation

Add confidence with:
- Sweep occurs during kill zone
- MSS after the sweep
- OB/FVG forms at sweep level
- HTF bias alignment

## 5. Mitigation Entry Model

Entering at mitigation blocks (areas of unmitigated orders).

### What is Mitigation?

When price moves through an area quickly, orders are left unfilled (unmitigated). Price often returns to "mitigate" these orders.

### Mitigation Block Identification

**Bullish Mitigation Block:**
- Bearish candle(s) that got engulfed/broken through
- Price rallied through this area
- Now acts as support when retested

**Bearish Mitigation Block:**
- Bullish candle(s) that got engulfed/broken through
- Price dropped through this area
- Now acts as resistance when retested

### Mitigation Entry Process

```
1. Identify rapid price movement (displacement)
2. Note candles that were "run through"
3. These candles = potential mitigation block
4. Wait for price to return to this area
5. Enter when price reaches mitigation block
6. Stop beyond the block
7. Target next liquidity
```

## 6. Inverse FVG Model

Using filled FVGs as entry zones.

### Inverse FVG Concept

When an FVG gets filled (price trades through it), it can become an entry zone in the opposite direction.

**Bullish Inverse FVG:**
1. Bearish FVG forms (gap down)
2. Price later fills the FVG (trades up through it)
3. The filled FVG now acts as support
4. Entry on retest of the filled FVG

**Bearish Inverse FVG:**
1. Bullish FVG forms (gap up)
2. Price later fills the FVG (trades down through it)
3. The filled FVG now acts as resistance
4. Entry on retest of the filled FVG

## Entry Precision Tips

### Optimal Entry Techniques

1. **Limit Orders at Level**
   - Place limit at OB/FVG 50%
   - Tighter stop, better R:R
   - May miss some trades

2. **Confirmation Entry**
   - Wait for LTF (1-5M) structure shift
   - Enter after confirmation candle
   - Higher hit rate, wider stop

3. **Scaled Entry**
   - Enter 50% at first touch
   - Enter 50% at 79% of zone
   - Average entry price

### Stop Loss Placement

| Entry Type | Stop Placement |
|-----------|----------------|
| OB Entry | Below OB low (bullish) / Above OB high (bearish) |
| FVG Entry | Below FVG (bullish) / Above FVG (bearish) |
| Unicorn | Below overlap zone |
| Turtle Soup | Below/above sweep wick |
| Mitigation | Beyond mitigation block |

### Position Management

1. **Partial at 1:1** - Secure some profit
2. **Move to BE** - After 1:1 or 2:1
3. **Trail with structure** - Move stop to each new swing
4. **Full exit at target** - Don't be greedy
