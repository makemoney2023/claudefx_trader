# ICT Silver Bullet Model

The Silver Bullet is a high-probability intraday setup that occurs during specific time windows when price displaces and creates a Fair Value Gap (FVG).

## Silver Bullet Definition

A Silver Bullet trade is:
- A **displacement** (aggressive price move)
- That creates a **Fair Value Gap**
- During a **specific time window**
- In the direction of the **daily bias**

## Silver Bullet Time Windows

There are THREE Silver Bullet windows each day:

### 1. London Silver Bullet
- **Time:** 3:00 AM - 4:00 AM EST
- **Best for:** EUR, GBP pairs
- **Context:** After London manipulation phase

### 2. New York AM Silver Bullet
- **Time:** 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM EST
- **Best for:** All major pairs, indices
- **Context:** After NY open manipulation

### 3. New York PM Silver Bullet
- **Time:** 2:00 PM - 3:00 PM EST
- **Best for:** USD pairs
- **Context:** Late session continuation or reversal

## Silver Bullet Entry Criteria

### Step 1: Determine Daily Bias
Before the Silver Bullet window:
- Analyze higher timeframe (H4, Daily)
- Identify key levels (PDH, PDL, weekly levels)
- Determine if day is bullish or bearish

### Step 2: Wait for Displacement
During the Silver Bullet window:
- Watch for aggressive price move (3+ candles in one direction)
- Move must be in the direction of your bias
- Displacement shows institutional commitment

### Step 3: Identify the FVG
The displacement creates:
- A Fair Value Gap (imbalance)
- This is your entry zone
- Gap should be "clean" (untested)

### Step 4: Entry on Retracement
- Price retraces back into the FVG
- Enter when price touches the FVG
- Use the FVG boundaries for risk management

### Step 5: Stop Loss and Target
- **Stop:** Below FVG (bullish) or above FVG (bearish)
- **Target:** Previous swing high/low, or PDH/PDL

## Silver Bullet Setup Checklist

```
PRE-SETUP (Before Silver Bullet Window):
[ ] Daily bias determined (bullish or bearish)
[ ] HTF key levels marked
[ ] No major news during window

DURING SILVER BULLET WINDOW:
[ ] Time is within Silver Bullet window
[ ] Displacement occurs in bias direction
[ ] FVG forms from displacement
[ ] FVG is clean (not previously tested)

ENTRY:
[ ] Price retraces to FVG
[ ] Entry at 50% of FVG (optimal)
[ ] Stop below/above FVG
[ ] R:R minimum 2:1
```

## Silver Bullet Filters

### High Probability Filters

Add these to increase win rate:

1. **Liquidity Sweep Before Displacement**
   - Price sweeps a key high/low before the Silver Bullet
   - Shows manipulation phase complete

2. **Order Block Confluence**
   - FVG overlaps with an order block
   - Multiple reasons for price to react

3. **Kill Zone Alignment**
   - Silver Bullet occurs during active kill zone
   - More volume = better follow-through

4. **No Immediate Resistance/Support**
   - Clear path to target
   - No major levels between entry and TP

### Low Probability Filters (Avoid)

1. **Counter-trend to HTF**
   - Silver Bullet against daily/weekly trend
   - Lower probability

2. **Major News Imminent**
   - Within 30 minutes of high-impact news
   - Unpredictable price action

3. **Extended Move Already**
   - Price already moved significantly
   - Limited upside remaining

4. **Wide FVG**
   - FVG > 15-20 pips on majors
   - Risk too large

## Multiple Silver Bullets

Sometimes multiple opportunities occur:

### Same Session Multiple Bullets
- First Silver Bullet at 10:15 AM
- Second Silver Bullet at 10:45 AM
- Trade the first, or wait for better entry

### Session Continuation
- London Silver Bullet sets direction
- NY Silver Bullet continues same direction
- Higher conviction on NY entry

## Silver Bullet vs Regular FVG Trade

| Aspect | Silver Bullet | Regular FVG |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Time | Specific windows only | Any time |
| Displacement | Required | Not required |
| Probability | Higher | Moderate |
| Filter | Time-based | Level-based |

## Risk Management for Silver Bullet

### Position Sizing
- Risk 1% of account per Silver Bullet
- Can increase to 1.5% if all filters align

### Stop Loss Rules
- Always below/above the FVG
- Add 2-3 pips buffer for spread
- Never move stop to "give room"

### Take Profit Rules
- First target: Previous swing (partial close)
- Final target: Daily liquidity level
- Move stop to breakeven after first target

## Summary

The Silver Bullet model combines:
1. **Time** (specific windows)
2. **Price** (FVG from displacement)
3. **Bias** (aligned with HTF)

This triple confluence creates high-probability setups that occur daily across multiple markets.
