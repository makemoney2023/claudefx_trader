# Precious Metals Trading Guide

## Overview

Precious metals (Gold - XAUUSD, Silver - XAGUSD) are unique trading instruments that combine safe-haven characteristics with significant trading opportunities. This guide covers ICT-style analysis adapted for precious metals.

## Gold (XAUUSD)

### Characteristics
- **Primary safe haven asset** - Institutions rotate to gold during uncertainty
- **Lower volatility** than silver (typically 1% daily range)
- **Strong USD inverse correlation** - Gold rises when USD falls
- **Central bank holdings** - Central banks are major holders and buyers
- **24-hour trading** - Continuous liquidity across sessions

### Trading Gold with ICT
1. **Market Structure** - Gold respects key swing levels; look for BOS/CHoCH
2. **Fair Value Gaps** - Less common but highly respected when formed
3. **Order Blocks** - Daily/4H OBs provide excellent entry zones
4. **Liquidity** - Watch for liquidity grabs at round numbers ($3,000, $2,900)
5. **Session Timing** - London and NY sessions have highest volume

### Stop Loss Guidelines
- **Minimum stop**: 30 pips ($30 per 0.01 lot)
- **ATR-based**: 1.5x 4H ATR recommended
- **Key level based**: Below nearest structural support

## Silver (XAGUSD)

### Characteristics
- **Higher volatility** - Typically 2x gold's daily range
- **Industrial demand** - Affected by manufacturing/tech sectors
- **More speculative** - Larger moves during momentum
- **1979 Pattern** - Historical parallel to current market (65% January gain)
- **Paper/Physical disconnect** - Physical premiums can signal divergence

### Key Price Levels (2026 - 1979 Pattern)

### The 1979 Pattern
In January 2026, silver gained 65% - only the 3rd time in 52 years this happened (after December 1979 and February 1974).

**1979 Sequence:**
1. December 1979: +65% initial surge
2. January 1980: +35-40% continuation
3. January 1980: Peak at $50
4. Crash: Back to $10 within months

**Trading Implications:**
- If pattern continues, potential for 35-40% additional gain
- Exit signals: Weekly RSI > 85, extreme bullish sentiment, euphoria phase
- Don't try to catch the exact top - take profits progressively

### Trading Silver with ICT
1. **Wider stops required** - 8-10% stop from entry typical
2. **Larger targets** - 2:1 or 3:1 R:R achievable
3. **Volume confirmation** - High volume on up days = accumulation
4. **Session bias** - NY session typically has highest volatility

## Gold/Silver Ratio Trading

### Understanding the Ratio
The gold/silver ratio measures how many ounces of silver it takes to buy one ounce of gold.

| Ratio Level | Interpretation | Trading Bias |
|-------------|----------------|--------------|
| > 100 | Extreme - Silver severely undervalued | Strong silver preference |
| 80-100 | High - Silver undervalued | Favor silver |
| 60-80 | Normal - Balanced | Trade both equally |
| 40-60 | Low - Silver may be overextended | Favor gold |
| < 40 | Extreme - Silver overvalued | Strong gold preference |

### Ratio Trading Strategy
1. **High ratio (>80)**: Open silver longs over gold longs
2. **Ratio compression**: When high ratio starts falling, silver is outperforming
3. **Confirmation**: Both metals rising = strong precious metals bid
4. **Divergence**: One metal leading may predict direction for other

## Safe Haven Analysis

### When Precious Metals Outperform
- **Geopolitical uncertainty** - Wars, tensions, sanctions
- **Currency debasement fears** - Inflation, money printing
- **Market stress** - Stock market crashes, credit events
- **USD weakness** - Dollar index declining

### Risk Assessment Framework
| Factor | Impact on Gold | Impact on Silver |
|--------|---------------|------------------|
| Geopolitical crisis | Strong positive | Positive |
| Inflation fears | Strong positive | Positive |
| USD weakness | Positive | Positive |
| Risk-off sentiment | Strong positive | Moderate positive |
| Industrial slowdown | Neutral | Negative |
| Interest rate hikes | Negative | Negative |

## Position Sizing for Precious Metals

### Gold (XAUUSD)
- **Standard forex position**: Use 70-80% of normal size
- **Pip value**: $1 per pip per 0.01 lot
- **Typical stop**: 30-50 pips
- **Risk per trade**: 1-2% of account recommended

### Silver (XAGUSD)
- **Standard forex position**: Use 50-60% of normal size (higher volatility)
- **Pip value**: Varies, approximately $5 per pip per 0.01 lot
- **Typical stop**: 200-500 pips
- **Risk per trade**: 1-2% of account, position adjusted for volatility

### Correlation Considerations
- Gold and silver correlation: ~0.90 (highly correlated)
- If holding both, consider them as **one position** for exposure calculation
- Avoid opening full-size positions in both simultaneously

## Session-Specific Notes

### Asian Session (Tokyo)
- Lower volume for precious metals
- Good for position accumulation
- Less likely to see major moves

### London Session
- Highest gold trading volume
- Often sets daily direction
- Watch London open for momentum

### New York Session
- Highest silver volatility
- Economic data releases impact both
- Most institutional activity

### Session Overlaps
- London/NY overlap (1300-1700 GMT): Highest overall volume
- Best time for entries with momentum confirmation

## Exit Strategies

### Gold Exit Signals
1. **RSI > 80** on daily/weekly - Overbought
2. **Failed ATH break** - Rejection at all-time high
3. **USD strength** - Dollar index breaking higher
4. **Risk-on rotation** - Money flowing back to stocks

### Silver Exit Signals (1979 Pattern Focus)
1. **Weekly RSI > 85** - Primary exit signal
2. **Extreme bullish sentiment** - "Everyone" bullish on silver
3. **Euphoria phase** - Price above $200
4. **Pattern completion** - 35-40% gain after initial surge

### Scaling Out Strategy
1. **TP1 (33%)**: At first resistance level
2. **TP2 (33%)**: At target 1 / pattern extension
3. **TP3 (34%)**: Trail stop for remainder

## Key Trading Rules

### For Gold
1. Always align with USD trend (inverse correlation)
2. Wider stops than forex - minimum 30 pips
3. Respect round number psychology ($2900, $3000, etc.)
4. Watch central bank announcements

### For Silver
1. Position size 50-60% of forex size due to volatility
2. 1979 pattern provides roadmap - use for targets
3. Exit discipline crucial - euphoria phase is dangerous
4. Monitor gold/silver ratio for relative value

### General
1. Never trade both at full size simultaneously
2. During high geopolitical risk, favor gold
3. During ratio compression, favor silver
4. Use session timing - London/NY overlap best
