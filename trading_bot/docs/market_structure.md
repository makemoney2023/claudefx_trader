# Market Structure Analysis

## Overview

Market structure is the foundation of ICT trading. It helps identify the current trend and potential reversal points by analyzing the sequence of swing highs and swing lows.

## Swing Points

### Swing High
A swing high forms when a candle's high is higher than the highs of the candles on both sides:
- Minimum 3-5 bars on each side for validation
- Represents a point where sellers overcame buyers
- Forms resistance levels

### Swing Low
A swing low forms when a candle's low is lower than the lows of the candles on both sides:
- Minimum 3-5 bars on each side for validation
- Represents a point where buyers overcame sellers
- Forms support levels

## Trend Identification

### Bullish Trend (Uptrend)
- Series of Higher Highs (HH) and Higher Lows (HL)
- Each swing high exceeds the previous swing high
- Each swing low is above the previous swing low
- **Trade bias**: Look for long opportunities

### Bearish Trend (Downtrend)
- Series of Lower Highs (LH) and Lower Lows (LL)
- Each swing high is below the previous swing high
- Each swing low breaks below the previous swing low
- **Trade bias**: Look for short opportunities

### Ranging/Consolidation
- No clear pattern of HH/HL or LH/LL
- Price oscillates between support and resistance
- **Trade bias**: Wait for breakout or trade range extremes

## Structure Breaks

### Break of Structure (BOS)
A continuation signal where price breaks a swing point in the direction of the trend:

**Bullish BOS**:
- Price breaks above a previous swing high
- Confirms bullish trend continuation
- Look for pullback to enter long

**Bearish BOS**:
- Price breaks below a previous swing low
- Confirms bearish trend continuation
- Look for pullback to enter short

### Change of Character (CHoCH)
The first sign of potential trend reversal:

**Bullish CHoCH**:
- During a downtrend, price breaks above a swing high
- First indication sellers are losing control
- Not yet confirmed reversal, but warning sign

**Bearish CHoCH**:
- During an uptrend, price breaks below a swing low
- First indication buyers are losing control
- Watch for follow-through to confirm

### Market Structure Shift (MSS)
A strong, impulsive break of structure indicating institutional involvement:

**Characteristics**:
- Large displacement candles (>1.5x ATR)
- Often leaves Fair Value Gap
- Strong momentum through the level
- Higher probability reversal signal than CHoCH

**Bullish MSS**:
- Strong break above swing high during downtrend
- Usually accompanied by bullish Order Block
- High probability long setup when price returns

**Bearish MSS**:
- Strong break below swing low during uptrend
- Usually accompanied by bearish Order Block
- High probability short setup when price returns

## Multi-Timeframe Structure

### Higher Timeframe (HTF)
- H4, Daily, Weekly
- Determines overall bias
- Only trade in HTF direction

### Lower Timeframe (LTF)
- M5, M15, H1
- Provides entry refinement
- Look for structure alignment with HTF

### Alignment Rules
1. Identify HTF trend (bullish/bearish)
2. Wait for LTF to show same directional bias
3. Enter on LTF structure break/confirmation
4. Set stops based on LTF structure

## Practical Application

### Identifying Valid Structure
1. Mark clear swing highs and lows
2. Look for minimum 3 bars separation
3. Ignore minor swings (noise)
4. Focus on significant price levels

### Trading Structure Breaks
1. Wait for candle body close beyond structure
2. Don't enter immediately on the break
3. Wait for pullback to broken level
4. Enter with stop beyond the structure

### Using Structure for Stops
- **Long trades**: Stop below recent swing low
- **Short trades**: Stop above recent swing high
- Add buffer for spread and volatility

## Common Patterns

### Internal Structure vs External Structure
- **External**: Major swing points visible on HTF
- **Internal**: Minor swings within the larger moves
- Trade external structure for higher probability

### Inducement
- False structure break designed to trap traders
- Price breaks structure but quickly reverses
- Often occurs before the real move

### Structure Liquidity
- Swing highs = buy stops (BSL)
- Swing lows = sell stops (SSL)
- Smart money targets these levels

## Key Rules

1. **Always identify HTF structure first**
2. **BOS = continuation, CHoCH = potential reversal**
3. **Wait for close beyond structure, not just wick**
4. **MSS is stronger signal than CHoCH**
5. **Combine with FVG/OB for higher probability**
6. **Structure on higher timeframe takes precedence**
