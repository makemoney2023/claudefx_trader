# Power of 3 / AMD (Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution)

## Overview

The Power of 3 describes the three phases of every market move according to ICT methodology. Understanding these phases helps traders avoid manipulation and enter with smart money.

## The Three Phases

### Phase 1: Accumulation
**Smart money building positions**

Characteristics:
- Range-bound, consolidating price action
- Low volatility
- Often looks "boring" or indecisive
- Creates liquidity at range highs and lows
- Retail traders get trapped in the range

What's happening:
- Institutions quietly buying/selling
- Building large positions without moving price
- Allowing retail to create liquidity pools

How to trade:
- Identify the range
- Mark range high and low
- Wait - don't trade the range
- Prepare for the next phases

### Phase 2: Manipulation (Judas Swing)
**Fake move to trap traders and sweep liquidity**

Characteristics:
- Sudden breakout from accumulation range
- Triggers stop losses
- Induces retail traders to enter wrong direction
- Quick, sharp move
- Often occurs at session opens

What's happening:
- Institutions sweep liquidity
- Trigger stops above/below range
- Trap early breakout traders
- Fill remaining positions at better prices

The Judas Swing:
- Named for its deceptive nature
- Initial move that "betrays" traders
- Goes opposite to the real move
- Classic manipulation tactic

How to trade:
- DON'T trade the initial breakout
- Wait for reversal signals
- This is NOT the real move
- Prepare for distribution phase

### Phase 3: Distribution
**The real move begins**

Characteristics:
- Strong momentum in true direction
- Often opposite to manipulation move
- Impulsive candles with follow-through
- Creates FVGs and order blocks
- Can trend for extended period

What's happening:
- Institutions now moving price to targets
- Retail who traded manipulation getting stopped out
- Real trend direction revealed
- Smart money distributing to late retail

How to trade:
- Enter after manipulation completes
- Look for structure break (CHoCH/MSS)
- Trade with the distribution move
- Target opposing liquidity

## Session Application

### Asian Session = Accumulation
- Price typically ranges
- Creates the "box" or consolidation
- Marks the manipulation targets (high/low)

### London Open = Manipulation
- Often sweeps Asian high OR low
- Initial move is the Judas swing
- Trap move before real direction

### London/NY = Distribution
- After manipulation completes
- Real move occurs
- Trends for the session

## Identifying Each Phase

### Signs of Accumulation
- Tight range for multiple candles
- Decreasing volatility
- Equal highs or lows forming
- No clear direction
- Low volume

### Signs of Manipulation
- Sudden breakout from range
- Spikes above/below range
- Quick reversal after breakout
- Long wicks (rejection)
- Occurs at session open

### Signs of Distribution
- Strong directional move
- Large body candles
- Multiple candles same direction
- Creating FVGs
- Breaking structure (BOS)

## Trading the Power of 3

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Identify Accumulation**
   - Mark the consolidation range
   - Note the highs and lows
   - These are manipulation targets

2. **Wait for Manipulation**
   - Watch for range break
   - DON'T enter on first breakout
   - This is likely the Judas swing

3. **Confirm Manipulation Complete**
   - Price sweeps liquidity
   - Reversal candle forms
   - Structure shifts (CHoCH)

4. **Enter on Distribution**
   - Trade opposite of manipulation
   - Enter at OB/FVG after reversal
   - Target opposing liquidity

### Entry Example (Bullish)

1. Asian session creates range (1.0800 - 1.0850)
2. London opens, sweeps below 1.0800 (manipulation down)
3. Price forms bullish engulfing at 1.0790
4. Structure shifts bullish (CHoCH)
5. Enter long on pullback to OB/FVG
6. Target: Above Asian high (1.0850+)

### Entry Example (Bearish)

1. Asian session creates range (1.0800 - 1.0850)
2. London opens, spikes above 1.0850 (manipulation up)
3. Price forms bearish engulfing at 1.0860
4. Structure shifts bearish (CHoCH)
5. Enter short on pullback to OB/FVG
6. Target: Below Asian low (1.0800-)

## Common Patterns

### Judas Swing Up → Real Move Down
1. Price breaks above resistance
2. Triggers buy stops
3. Traps breakout longs
4. Reverses sharply
5. Trends down for the day

### Judas Swing Down → Real Move Up
1. Price breaks below support
2. Triggers sell stops
3. Traps breakout shorts
4. Reverses sharply
5. Trends up for the day

## Combining with Other Concepts

### Power of 3 + Liquidity
- Manipulation phase sweeps liquidity
- Distribution targets opposing liquidity
- Natural confluence

### Power of 3 + Kill Zones
- Accumulation: Asian session
- Manipulation: London/NY open
- Distribution: London/NY session

### Power of 3 + Order Blocks
- OBs form during distribution
- Enter at OBs for continuation
- High probability trades

## Key Rules

1. **Don't trade accumulation**
   - Wait for the cycle to complete
   - Range trading is low probability

2. **Don't trade the Judas**
   - First move is often fake
   - Wait for confirmation

3. **Trade distribution**
   - This is where money is made
   - Enter after manipulation

4. **Use session timing**
   - Asian = accumulation likely
   - Session opens = manipulation likely

5. **Confirm with structure**
   - CHoCH confirms manipulation end
   - BOS confirms distribution

## Mistakes to Avoid

1. Trading the first breakout (Judas trap)
2. Forcing trades during accumulation
3. Not waiting for manipulation to complete
4. Fighting the distribution trend
5. Ignoring session context
