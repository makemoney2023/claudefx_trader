# Kill Zones (Trading Sessions)

## Overview

Kill Zones are specific time windows during the trading day when institutional activity is highest. These periods offer the best trading opportunities due to increased liquidity and more predictable price behavior.

## Session Times (EST/New York Time)

### Asian Session
- **Time**: 7:00 PM - 12:00 AM EST
- **Characteristics**:
  - Lower volatility
  - Range-bound price action
  - Accumulation/distribution phase
  - Creates liquidity at session extremes
- **Trading Approach**:
  - Generally avoid trading
  - Mark the range (high/low)
  - These levels become targets for London

### London Kill Zone ⭐
- **Time**: 2:00 AM - 5:00 AM EST
- **Characteristics**:
  - First major session
  - Often sweeps Asian highs OR lows
  - Creates the "manipulation" move
  - Real direction often opposite of initial move
- **Trading Approach**:
  - Wait for Asian level sweep
  - Look for reversal after sweep
  - High probability setups

### London Session (Extended)
- **Time**: 2:00 AM - 11:00 AM EST
- **Characteristics**:
  - Major liquidity
  - Trend setting session
  - Best for GBP, EUR pairs
- **Trading Approach**:
  - Trade with established direction
  - Use pullbacks for entry
  - Target liquidity pools

### New York Kill Zone ⭐
- **Time**: 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM EST
- **Characteristics**:
  - Highest volume period
  - Overlap with London
  - Often continues London direction
  - Or creates major reversal
- **Trading Approach**:
  - Best trading window
  - Look for continuation setups
  - Major economic news releases

### London Close
- **Time**: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM EST
- **Characteristics**:
  - Profit taking period
  - Potential reversals
  - Reduced momentum
  - Position squaring
- **Trading Approach**:
  - Look for reversal setups
  - Scale out of positions
  - Avoid new trends

### Off Hours
- **Time**: 12:00 PM - 7:00 PM EST
- **Characteristics**:
  - Low liquidity
  - Choppy price action
  - Unpredictable moves
  - Wider spreads
- **Trading Approach**:
  - Generally avoid trading
  - Prepare for next session
  - Analysis time

## Session Flow

### Typical Daily Sequence

1. **Asian (7 PM - 12 AM)**
   - Range forms
   - Liquidity builds at extremes
   - Low volatility

2. **London Open (2 AM - 5 AM)**
   - Volatility increases
   - Often sweeps Asian high OR low
   - "Judas Swing" (fake move)
   - Real move begins

3. **London Continuation (5 AM - 7 AM)**
   - Trend established
   - Pullback entries
   - Building momentum

4. **NY Open (7 AM - 10 AM)**
   - Maximum volatility
   - Continuation or reversal
   - Best setups
   - News releases

5. **London Close (10 AM - 12 PM)**
   - Profit taking
   - Potential reversals
   - Momentum fading

6. **Afternoon (12 PM - 7 PM)**
   - Low activity
   - Avoid trading
   - Prepare for next day

## Kill Zone Strategies

### London Kill Zone Strategy

**Setup 1: Asian Range Sweep**
1. Mark Asian session high and low
2. Wait for London to sweep one level
3. Look for reversal pattern
4. Enter opposite direction
5. Target opposing Asian level (or beyond)

**Setup 2: London Judas Swing**
1. Note initial London direction
2. Wait for it to fail/reverse
3. Enter on reversal confirmation
4. "Judas" was the fake move

### New York Kill Zone Strategy

**Setup 1: London Continuation**
1. London established clear direction
2. Price pulls back in NY open
3. Enter on pullback at OB/FVG
4. Target new highs/lows
5. Trail stop below structure

**Setup 2: NY Reversal**
1. London made extended move
2. Price at key level in NY open
3. Look for reversal signals
4. Enter against London move
5. Target London origin

### London Close Strategy

**Reversal Setup**
1. Strong trending day
2. Price extended at London close
3. Look for exhaustion signals
4. Enter reversal trade
5. Target intraday levels

## Session-Specific Tips

### For London Session
- Focus on EUR and GBP pairs
- Watch for Asian level sweeps
- Be patient for the real move
- News from Europe affects direction

### For New York Session
- All pairs active
- News releases cause volatility
- Wait for news to settle
- Best R:R opportunities

### For Asian Session
- USD/JPY can be active
- Good for range trading if experienced
- Mark levels for future sessions
- Generally better to rest

## Economic News Considerations

### High Impact News
- NFP (Non-Farm Payroll)
- FOMC/Interest Rate Decisions
- CPI/Inflation Data
- GDP Reports

### Trading Around News
- Avoid entering 30 min before
- Wait for volatility to settle (15-30 min after)
- Or trade the reaction with wider stops
- News can create sweep opportunities

## Kill Zone Rules

1. **Only trade during Kill Zones**
   - London KZ: 2-5 AM EST
   - NY KZ: 7-10 AM EST
   - London Close: 10 AM-12 PM (reversals)

2. **Asian session = Preparation**
   - Mark the range
   - Identify liquidity
   - Set alerts

3. **Wait for the sweep**
   - London sweeps Asian levels
   - NY may sweep London levels
   - Don't anticipate, wait

4. **First hour is manipulation**
   - Initial moves often fake
   - Wait for confirmation
   - "Judas Swing" concept

5. **Combine with ICT concepts**
   - Kill Zone + Liquidity Sweep + OB/FVG
   - Multiple confluence = high probability

## Common Mistakes

1. Trading during Asian session
2. Entering on first London move (often fake)
3. Trading after London close
4. Ignoring news release times
5. Not adjusting for time zones
6. Fighting established trends within Kill Zone
