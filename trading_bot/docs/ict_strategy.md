# ICT (Inner Circle Trading) Strategy

## Overview

ICT (Inner Circle Trading) is a comprehensive trading methodology developed by Michael J. Huddleston that focuses on understanding and trading alongside institutional order flow. The core premise is that retail traders can profit by identifying where large institutions are likely to enter and exit the market.

## Core Principles

### 1. Smart Money Concepts
- Large institutions (banks, hedge funds) move the market
- They need liquidity to fill their large orders
- They engineer price moves to access this liquidity
- Understanding their footprint allows us to trade alongside them

### 2. Time and Price
- Certain times of day have higher institutional activity (Kill Zones)
- Price levels where orders cluster are significant
- Combining time and price creates high-probability setups

## Strategy Framework

### Step 1: Determine Higher Timeframe Bias
Before looking for entries, establish the dominant trend:
- Use H4 or Daily timeframe
- Identify if market is making Higher Highs + Higher Lows (Bullish)
- Or Lower Highs + Lower Lows (Bearish)
- Only take trades in the direction of HTF bias

### Step 2: Wait for Kill Zone
Trade only during high-probability sessions:
- **London Kill Zone**: 2:00 AM - 5:00 AM EST
- **New York Kill Zone**: 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM EST
- **London Close**: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM EST (for reversals)

### Step 3: Identify Liquidity
Before the real move, smart money often:
- Sweeps obvious highs/lows to trigger stop losses
- Takes out equal highs/lows
- Hunts retail traders' stops

### Step 4: Find Entry at Premium/Discount
- **Bullish**: Enter at discount (price below 50% of range)
- **Bearish**: Enter at premium (price above 50% of range)
- Entry zones: Order Blocks or Fair Value Gaps

### Step 5: Execute with Risk Management
- Stop loss beyond structure (swing high/low)
- Minimum 1:2 risk-reward ratio
- Take profit at opposing liquidity

## Entry Criteria Checklist

For a LONG trade:
1. ☐ HTF trend is bullish (HH + HL)
2. ☐ Currently in London or NY Kill Zone
3. ☐ Sell-side liquidity has been swept (stop hunt below lows)
4. ☐ Price is at a bullish Order Block or FVG
5. ☐ MSS or CHoCH confirms bullish momentum on LTF
6. ☐ Risk-reward is minimum 1:2

For a SHORT trade:
1. ☐ HTF trend is bearish (LH + LL)
2. ☐ Currently in London or NY Kill Zone
3. ☐ Buy-side liquidity has been swept (stop hunt above highs)
4. ☐ Price is at a bearish Order Block or FVG
5. ☐ MSS or CHoCH confirms bearish momentum on LTF
6. ☐ Risk-reward is minimum 1:2

## Trade Management

### Entry Execution
- Use limit orders at OB/FVG zones
- Or market orders after LTF confirmation

### Stop Loss Placement
- Below/above the Order Block
- Below/above recent swing structure
- Add buffer for spread

### Take Profit Targets
- **TP1**: Opposing liquidity (nearest EQH/EQL)
- **TP2**: Next significant structure level
- Consider partial profits at TP1

### Position Management
- Move to break-even after 1R profit
- Trail stop below/above structure
- Close partial at key levels

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Trading against HTF trend** - Always align with bigger picture
2. **Entering without liquidity sweep** - Wait for the trap
3. **Ignoring Kill Zones** - Low probability outside these times
4. **Chasing price** - Wait for price to come to your zone
5. **Poor risk-reward** - Never below 1:2
6. **Overtrading** - Quality over quantity

## Example Setup (Long)

1. Daily chart shows bullish structure (HH + HL)
2. It's 8:00 AM EST (NY Kill Zone)
3. Price swept yesterday's low (took sell-side liquidity)
4. Price is now at a 1H bullish Order Block
5. 15M shows bullish CHoCH (change of character)
6. Entry at OB, SL below swing low, TP at previous high
7. Risk-reward is 1:3

## Key Terminology Quick Reference

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **BOS** | Break of Structure - continuation signal |
| **CHoCH** | Change of Character - first sign of reversal |
| **MSS** | Market Structure Shift - strong reversal |
| **FVG** | Fair Value Gap - imbalance zone |
| **OB** | Order Block - institutional entry zone |
| **BSL** | Buy-Side Liquidity - stops above highs |
| **SSL** | Sell-Side Liquidity - stops below lows |
| **EQH** | Equal Highs - liquidity pool |
| **EQL** | Equal Lows - liquidity pool |
| **OTE** | Optimal Trade Entry - 62-79% retracement |
